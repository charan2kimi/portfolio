How the pieces fit

System-context view. The document AI and agent platform is the product: the document AI pipeline, the agent harness, the safety layer, and the serving layer, deployed in the customer's own environment. DevX is where a firm catalogs its APIs and authors the agents and tools the platform runs. The fine-tuning platform supplies private models to the serving layer. Forum Sentry sits outside the loop, seven years of gateway engineering the AI stack inherits its instincts from.
