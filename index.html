Ecosystem: one control plane, one runtime, one foundation

System-context view. DevX is the umbrella developer platform where APIs, tools, agents, evaluations, and the full model lifecycle are authored, governed, and published. The Agent Execution Platform is the separate realtime runtime that executes what DevX publishes. Forum Sentry is not part of this loop: it sits lateral to it, the seven years of gateway and governance engineering the AI platforms inherit.
